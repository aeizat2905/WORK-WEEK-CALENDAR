ADI and ASE Work Weeks

Both calendars run Sunday to Saturday. Each fiscal year has 4 quarters of 13 weeks (3 months each), and 52 or 53 weeks in total. In a 53-week year, Q4 has 14 weeks.

What this is based on

  • ADI FY ends on the Saturday closest to 31 Oct. ASE FY ends on the Saturday closest to 31 Dec. That is why some years have 53 weeks (ADI FY29 and ASE FY25 in this calendar).
